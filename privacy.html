Novera Bot — Privacy Policy Last updated: October 18, 2026 1. Introduction Novera is a Discord bot that provides community features, utilities, profiles, a virtual currency system, events, support tickets, and AI-powered responses. This Privacy Policy explains what information Novera may access, how it is used, which third-party services process it, and how users can ask questions about their data. 2. Information We May Process Depending on the features used and the server's configuration, Novera may process: - Discord account information: User IDs, usernames, server membership information, and other information needed to identify users interacting with the bot. - Messages and commands: Messages used to trigger bot commands or interact with Novera's AI features. - AI conversation context: Conversation messages supplied to the AI service to help generate relevant responses and maintain conversational context. - Bot feature data: Information associated with profiles, virtual currency, AFK status, events, giveaways, and other features. - Support ticket information: Messages and details submitted through the bot's ticket system. - Technical information: Information made available through Discord or third-party services as needed to operate and troubleshoot the bot. The precise information processed depends on which features are used. 3. How Information Is Used Information may be processed to: - Respond to commands and mentions. - Generate AI responses and provide relevant conversational context. - Operate profiles, virtual currency, events, giveaways, and other bot features. - Provide support through the ticket system. - Maintain functionality, troubleshoot errors, and prevent misuse. Novera does not intend to use Discord information for unrelated advertising or sell users' personal information. 4. AI Processing and Groq Novera uses Groq's AI service to generate AI-powered responses. When users interact with the AI feature, relevant messages and conversation context may be sent to Groq for processing. This content may include information users voluntarily share in their messages. Groq processes submitted inputs to provide its service. Its own privacy terms and data-handling practices apply to information processed through its service. Groq states that inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default, although limited retention may occur for service reliability or abuse monitoring. Actual handling depends on the applicable service settings and policies. Users should avoid sharing passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, or other sensitive personal information with the AI feature. 5. Storage and Retention Novera uses conversation context for its AI functionality. The exact retention period and deletion behavior for AI-related information have not yet been fully confirmed. Other information associated with bot features may be handled separately according to how those features operate. Novera's retention and deletion practices should be reviewed and updated as these details are confirmed. Users may contact the Novera team to ask about information associated with their use of the bot. 6. Sharing With Third Parties Novera may send relevant AI inputs to Groq to generate responses. Information may also be processed by Discord as part of normal bot operation. Information is not intended to be sold or shared for unrelated advertising. Any additional sharing should be limited to what is necessary for the bot's functionality, legal obligations, or other applicable permitted purposes. 7. Data Requests and Deletion Users may contact the Novera team to ask what information is associated with their use of the bot or to request deletion where applicable. Requests can be submitted through the Novera Discord server: https://discord.gg/xxjT3Pq4Zt Requests may require sufficient information to identify the relevant data. Some information may remain with third-party service providers under their own applicable retention policies or where retention is legally required. 8. Data Security Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information handled by Novera against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security. 9. Children's Privacy Novera is intended to be used in accordance with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. Users should not submit sensitive personal information through the bot or its AI features. 10. Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as Novera's features and data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL. 11.
